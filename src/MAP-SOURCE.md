The map outline is extracted from Natural Earth 1:50m admin-0 countries:
https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_50m_admin_0_countries.geojson
Natural Earth data is public domain: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

Only the Vietnam geometry is retained in vietnam-outline.json. The build projects
its geographic coordinates into the SVG canvas. Decorative contour strokes are
illustrations, not a topographic or road dataset. Region colors classify travel
locations, not administrative boundaries. The map is a travel overview, with a
visible note about omitted islands and borders. Destination coordinates are for
orientation; Google Maps links search the named destination. Hội An's marker is
offset with a leader line so it can be selected separately from nearby Đà Nẵng.
